HONEYCRISP: HOW TO INSTALL

You need: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) on macOS 15 or later, and Minecraft Java Edition.
You only need the one file: honeycrisp-0.1.1.jar. Don't unzip it, and don't double-click it.


OPTION A: Modrinth App (easiest)
1. Download the Modrinth App from modrinth.com/app and sign in with your Microsoft account.
2. Make a new instance: Minecraft 26.3, loader Fabric.
3. Open the instance and drag honeycrisp-0.1.1.jar onto its content page.
4. Press Play.


OPTION B: Prism Launcher
1. Download Prism Launcher from prismlauncher.org and add your Microsoft account.
2. Add Instance: Minecraft 26.3, then choose Fabric under Mod Loader.
3. Right-click the instance, choose Edit, open Mods, and drag the jar in.
4. Press Launch. If it asks about Java, let it download Java 25 for you.


OPTION C: The normal Minecraft Launcher
1. Install Java if you don't have it (adoptium.net). The Fabric installer needs it.
2. Get the Fabric installer from fabricmc.net/use. Choose "Download installer (Universal/.JAR)".
   Close Minecraft, open the installer (if macOS blocks it, right-click it and choose Open),
   pick 26.3, and click Install.
3. In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G and go to:
      ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft
   Make a folder called "mods" if there isn't one, and put the jar in it.
4. Open the Minecraft Launcher, pick the "fabric-loader-26.3" profile, and press Play.


SHADER SETTINGS: OFF, LOW, HIGH
In the game, go to Options > Video Settings. The first button under the graphics preset is
"Shaders". Click it to switch between:
- High: everything at full quality.
- Low: smaller shadow maps, coarser fog, and the world drawn at a lower resolution.
  Much lighter on the graphics chip, with most of the look.
- Off: Minecraft's normal look, still drawn with Metal.
The change applies after you quit and restart the game.

Which one to pick: Low on A-series or lower-tier M chips, High on everything else.
A-series Macs start on Low by themselves; all others start on High.


TIPS
- Shaders are on automatically. You don't need OptiFine or Iris.
- Install it on its own first, without other graphics mods like Sodium or Iris.
- It works on any server (it only changes your graphics).
- For a new version, just swap the old jar for the new one.
- Something wrong? Send me a screenshot plus the file logs/latest.log from your
  instance or minecraft folder.
